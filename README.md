## Backend utility

Within the universe of this project, Flask backend, altogether with MySQL database, has serve to authenticate the only person who has permission to access the project's hidden routes, as well as to give them the ability of creating, alterating and deleting the project's news and to give the readers access to the aforementioned published news.
